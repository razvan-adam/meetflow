S2 · Pending-tasks dashboard

Journey J5 · MF-21, MF-18

Rules shown

  • Landing page after login.
  • Only my open and in-progress tasks, across all teams, soonest deadline first.
  • Overdue = not done and past the deadline. Highlighted, not a separate status.

  • Each task links to its meeting's minutes.

S7 · Live meeting

Journey J4 · MF-15, MF-16, MF-17, MF-18

Rules shown

  • Open commitments are reviewed first. Owner or organizer updates status.
  • Decisions belong to an agenda item. Tasks are created under a decision or item.
  • Description, owner and deadline are all required. Owner list = this meeting's participants.
  • Closing asks for confirmation and says what will happen. After closing everything locks except task status.

S1 · Sign up / log in

Journeys J1, J2 · MF-8, MF-10

Rules shown

  • Opened from an invitation: the team and inviter are named, and the email is fixed.

E1–E3 · Emails

Journeys J2, J5 · MF-10, MF-22, MF-20

Rules shown

  • E1: single-use link, expiry date stated.
  • E2: sent to every participant on close. A short summary that links to the full minutes.
  • E3: one email per owner per meeting, only their tasks from that meeting. Cannot be turned off, no reminders.
  • No data from teams the recipient doesn't belong to.
