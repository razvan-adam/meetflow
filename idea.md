# MeetFlow – Product Idea

## The problem

Companies hold many meetings, but much of what is agreed in them is lost afterwards. Decisions are not written down, tasks have no clear owner or deadline, and nobody checks at the next meeting whether they were done. The same topics come up again and again, and commitments quietly slip.

## The solution

MeetFlow is a web application that helps companies run their meetings and, above all, follow through on what is decided in them.

It is not a note-taking app. Its focus is **commitment tracking**: every decision or task agreed in a meeting gets an owner and a deadline, the owner receives reminders, and the item automatically reappears at the next meeting until it is completed.

## How it works

1. **Before the meeting** – create the meeting, invite participants and build the agenda. Open tasks from the previous meeting are carried over automatically.
2. **During the meeting** – record the topics discussed, the decisions made and the tasks agreed, each with an owner and a deadline.
3. **After the meeting** – structured minutes are generated and sent to participants. Owners get reminders as deadlines approach and update the status of their tasks.
4. **At the next meeting** – pending commitments come back on the agenda, so nothing is forgotten.

## Target customers

- Small and medium-sized businesses
- Operations, logistics and administration departments
- Consulting firms

## MVP features

1. User and team sign-up
2. Meeting creation
3. Recording of agreements
4. Task assignment with owners
5. Pending-tasks dashboard
6. Meeting history
7. Minutes generation

## Future features

- Microsoft Teams and calendar integrations
- Advanced automations
- Team statistics and analytics
